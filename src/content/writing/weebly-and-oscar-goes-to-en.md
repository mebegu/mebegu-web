Yesterday, the 85th Academy Awards found their winners in Los Angeles, America. You can find who won in each category at the end of my post.

Didn't this year's Academy Awards seem to reek of American nationalism? I am not saying that I am against American nationalism. I would simply have preferred these awards, which attract so much attention around the world, to be handed out a little more impartially.

Looking at the list, we see that Ben Affleck's Argo won Best Picture. The film is about an operation by American soldiers to rescue 52 Americans taken hostage during the days of the revolution in Iran. Considering America's tensions with Iran, that struck me as rather telling. The fact that the award was announced by Michelle Obama herself is another matter, in my opinion. Looking at Best Actor, we see that Daniel Day-Lewis won for playing Abraham Lincoln, one of America's most important presidents. In addition, Django and Skyfall, which also won awards, stood out to me as America-focused films.

Looking at the winning films overall, Life of Pi stands out with four awards; Les Miserables and Argo with three; and Lincoln, Django and Skyfall with two each.

My own hope for Best Picture this year was Life of Pi, but things did not turn out as I had hoped. Still, I congratulate all of the award-winning films.

The awards  
**Best Picture:** "Argo"  
**Best Actress:** Jennifer Lawrence, "Silver Linings Playbook"  
**Best Actor:** Daniel Day-Lewis, "Lincoln"  
**Best Director:** Ang Lee, "Life of Pi"  
**Best Supporting Actress:** Anne Hathaway, "Les Miserables"  
**Best Supporting Actor:** Christoph Waltz, "Django Unchained"  
**Best Adapted Screenplay:** "Argo", Chris Terrio  
**Best Original Screenplay:** "Django Unchained", Quentin Tarantino  
**Best Cinematography:** Claudio Miranda, "Life of Pi"  
**Best Documentary:** "Searching For Sugar Man", Malik Bendjelloul and Simon Chinn  
**Best Film Editing:** "Argo", William Goldenberg  
**Best Foreign Language Film:** "Amour" (Austria)  
**Best Animated Film:** "Brave", Mark Andrews and Brenda Chapman  
**Best Original Score:** "Life of Pi", Mychael Danna  
**Best Original Song:** "Skyfall", Adele Adkins and Paul Epworth  
**Best Production Design:** "Lincoln"  
**Best Animated Short Film:** "Paperman", John Kahrs  
**Best Short Film:** "Curfew", Shawn Christensen  
**Best Documentary Short:** "Inocente", Sean Fine and Andrea Nix Fine  
**Best Sound Editing:** "Skyfall", Per Hallberg and Karen Baker Landers, and "Zero Dark Thirty", Paul N.J. Ottosson  
**Best Sound Mixing:** "Les Miserables", Andy Nelson, Mark Paterson and Simon Hayes  
**Best Costume Design:** "Anna Karenina", Jacqueline Durran  
**Best Makeup and Hairstyling:** Lisa Westcott and Julie Dartnell, "Les Miserables"  
**Best Visual Effects:** "Life of Pi", Bill Westenhofer, Guillaume Rocheron, Erik-Jan De Boer and Donald R. Elliott.
