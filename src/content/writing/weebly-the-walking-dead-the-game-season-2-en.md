*The Walking Dead: The Game, which left its mark on 2012, will be back very soon with its second season. So what awaits us in season two?*

![](/media/weebly/1383513421-7615b0f4fa.png)

The Walking Dead, the much-loved comic book and television series, entered the digital gaming world in 2012. And what an entrance it made. With its music, atmosphere and story, the game won over almost everyone who played it and received more than 90 awards.[5] The first season consisted of five episodes, and its shocking finale caught many players off guard.

Most recently, it returned with an expansion called 400 Days in July. Details about the second season of The Walking Dead began to emerge in the past few days.

![](/media/weebly/1383515594-234681365a.png)

Telltale Games announced that we will continue Clementine's story in the second season. It was also announced that we will control Clementine this time.[1][4] In the first game, we controlled Lee and tried to help Clem. As an adult, Lee was more mature, level-headed and responsible than Clementine. Let's see whether we can build a strong bond with a less experienced character like Clem in season two.

Telltale Games will most likely make changes to the gameplay as well. Situations that physically push the character and confrontations with zombies requiring brute strength, as we experienced when playing Lee, will probably give way to more puzzle-focused and run-and-hide challenges in the second season. Personally, I think playing as Clem after a character like Lee will breathe new life into the game.

![](/media/weebly/1383515567-941ba48ab4.png)

In interviews, the game's developers said that anything could happen in the game and that they had no rigid boundaries. When asked whether we might encounter characters from the television series or the comics, they said that they were open to all kinds of developments in the story, so why not in later episodes?[2] It was also confirmed at Telltale's Comic-Con 2013 panel that we would encounter Kenny, whom we know from the first game. Another detail we have is that the second season takes place months after the first, and that our choices in the 400 Days expansion, as well as those in the first season, will affect the story.[3]

![](/media/weebly/1383516841-0519d4b715.png)

> "Keep that hair short. I'll miss you" -Lee Everett

![](/media/weebly/1383516985-382c0587f6.jpg)

The sequel, whose first episode is expected to be released towards the end of November, is currently available for preorder on different platforms. The game consists of five episodes, which will reach players at intervals of four to six weeks. In other words, season two will be over by the middle of 2014. Let's see what events we encounter in the sequel.[1][3][4]

One final reminder concerns the Game of the Year edition of the first game. The Walking Dead: Game of the Year, which includes the first game and the 400 Days expansion, will be available to players before the end of this year.[4]
